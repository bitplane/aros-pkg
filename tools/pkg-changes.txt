A word that is not a verb is now named, and answered in one sentence instead of a page of usage. When it is a slip of the fingers, the verb it is nearest to is offered: INSTAL gives "did you mean INSTALL?", whatever case it was typed in.
When the word is near no verb at all, the refusal says which build does not know it and that a later build may, since a verb an older build has never heard of is what usually brings a person here. Verbs and keywords have never been case sensitive, in any build.
