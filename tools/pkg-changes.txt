Pkg reads and pushes channels over https on AROS, as it always has on macOS, Linux and Windows: write the portal's address with https:// there too. The AROS builds carry the TLS library and a list of certificate authorities, so nothing is installed beside Pkg, and they check the certificate against the address every time, with no way to turn that off; a machine behind its own authority names its certificates in PKG_CAFILE. Plain http:// is still read, for a channel you serve yourself. KEYGEN takes its randomness from the system on an AROS that has one, and only asks you to type when it does not. Carrying TLS makes the AROS program about twice its old size, around 1.5 MB.
