PUSH works from AROS: to an http:// address, or with no portal key, each request is signed with the publisher's own key, and the portal knows the publisher by its public half. Nothing secret travels, so it needs no TLS. Over https with PKG_PUSHKEY nothing changes.
CHECKSIG <file> FILE <sigfile> checks a signature SIGN made and says whose it is, with nothing but Pkg: on AROS there is no ssh-keygen.
A download shows its progress at a terminal or a Shell window: downloading 12.4 of 640.0 MB.
